Wireless Micro Lightning KitはiPhone 15で使える？

microphone

条件付き対応

Wireless Micro Lightning Kitは、iPhone 15ではLightning受信機を直接接続できませんが、Bluetooth Direct ConnectをRØDE Captureで使用できます。

利用条件

接続

  • Lightning receiver cannot connect directly to iPhone 15 USB-C port
  • Bluetooth Direct Connect

firmware / system

  • Firmware v2.08 or later

用途制約

  • RØDE Capture app only

接続情報

根拠・公式情報

  • Can I Use the Wireless Micro Lightning Kit with a USB-C Mobile Device?
    For iPhone 15 and above, the Lightning receiver is not physically compatible with USB-C. Direct Connect connects the transmitters to the USB-C iPhone over Bluetooth, exclusively with RØDE Capture, and requires firmware v2.08 or later.
