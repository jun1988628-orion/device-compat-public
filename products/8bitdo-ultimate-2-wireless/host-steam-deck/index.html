Ultimate 2 Wireless is officially supported with SteamOS after the documented firmware and Bluetooth-mode requirements.

利用条件

  • Use SteamOS 3.7.13 or later.
  • Update controller firmware to v1.05 or later.
  • Use Bluetooth mode and pair from the SteamOS Bluetooth settings.
