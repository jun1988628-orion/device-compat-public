条件付き対応

8BitDo Wireless USB Adapter is officially documented for Steam Deck in Xinput mode with SteamOS Holo 3.4+.

利用条件

  • Update Steam Deck and use adapter Xinput mode.
  • SteamOS Holo 3.4+ is required.
